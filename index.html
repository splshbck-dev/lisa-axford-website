The journey that led me here

She's navigated boardrooms, oceans, and everything in between.

For over twenty-five years, Lisa led supply chain and operations teams through some of the highest-pressure environments in global business — multi-million-dollar budgets, cross-continental teams, and the kind of change that would rattle most people.

Long before that, she was a yacht skipper, navigating two Trans-Atlantic crossings and training crews to sail through open water, uncertainty, and the occasional hurricane-force wind.

What she learned in both worlds is the same: real transformation isn't about the destination, it's about learning to read your own compass again. Today she combines that leadership experience with her passion for personal transformation, helping women remember their strength, embrace intentional change, and create lives they truly love.

    Frequently asked

    Before you book

    What actually happens on a Clarity Call?+

    It's a focused 30–40 minute conversation, not a sales pitch. We talk through where you are, what's not working, and what you're really working toward. By the end, you'll know whether The Emergence Program is the right next step — and if it's not, I'll tell you that too.

    Is this coaching or therapy?+

    Coaching. Therapy looks backward, toward healing. Coaching looks forward, toward action. We'll occasionally touch on beliefs that are holding you back, but the focus stays on building the plan that gets you from here to there.

    Will this be a good fit for me?+

    If you're high-functioning on paper but feel quietly disconnected from your own life — if you sense there's more waiting for you but can't quite name it — this is built for you.

    How much time does the program take?+

    We'll map out the exact time commitment on your Clarity Call, based on the format that fits your life — most women find the weekly rhythm takes less time than the energy they were spending feeling stuck.

    What results can I expect?+

    A clear, motivating vision of your future self. A plan built around it. And the accountability and community to actually walk it out — not just think about it.
